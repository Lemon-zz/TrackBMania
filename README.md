**TrackBmania** is a free, open-source trackball project.

## Overview

Basically i made it myself, as i find no commercial alternatives - i had Kensington and find it a little discomfy - so i made one for me.

As controller i use ESP32-S3 - it have built-in USB for programming and work, along with wireless capacbility.

And i want it to have smooth scroll - and i made it. Now i scroll not by 3 rows, but pixel-by-pixel.

I printed it with Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra.

BOM:

So, to assembly full device you will need:

1. PCB with soldered components (obviously) - look here https://github.com/Lemon-zz/TrackBMania/blob/main/BOM
2. Printables  - Look in 3D folder:
  1. Case
  2. Buttons
  3. Ball holder and bearing separator
  4. 2 parts of wheel/gear.
7. 3x BTU QX319 (easily find on ali)
8. 10 x MR42  2x4x2(mm) chrome steel Miniature Bearings
9. M2.5 x 8 Screws
10. 57.2mm classic billiard ball

Also i recommend use of M6x1.0 thread cutter - not neccessary but makes installing BTUs much easier.
